Status: Approved visual direction, 4 November 2026. Production assets require geometry and print validation.

## Authority
The WPU logo shown on worldpickleball.world and supplied by the brand owner is the authoritative primary logo. Never substitute generated recreations for it. Existing public/brand/wpu-logo-approved.png is used by the site header; compare its pixels against the supplied official logo before replacing it. Generated boards are reference mockups, not source artwork.

## Certification system
Use one fixed WPU APPROVED shield across all equipment and media. The NFC/QR tag and verification page reuse the same mark. A badge is not evidence of current approval: the verification record is authoritative. Model-level certification records should include model identity, scope, issue and expiry dates, current status, and an auditable change history. States: active, expired, suspended, revoked. Do not display working QR codes, claims of live verification or third-party endorsements until deployed and authorised.

## Governance of assets
Keep versioned source SVGs and exported transparent PNGs under public/brand/ once verified. Archive superseded files separately; do not overwrite approved masters without review. Changes require review of geometry, contrast, trademark usage, print legibility, accessibility and responsive website presentation.

## Public copy safeguards
WPU is being established; do not assert governing-body recognition or operational certification capabilities without formal confirmation. Technical partnerships and testing claims must be verified before publication.
