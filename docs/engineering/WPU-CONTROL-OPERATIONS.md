# WPU Control operations workspace

- The overview, network directory and organisation activity are available to staff who can both review intake and publish federations. A global WPU administrator can hold both permissions. Intake-only staff start in the review queue and can follow an approved application to its linked record. Federation operators start in their own approved workspace and cannot browse the WPU network.
- All views remain on the Cloudflare Access-protected /control route. Existing WPU session, verified identity, role checks, decision endpoints, and audit events remain authoritative. The redesign adds no database migration and does not change Access settings.
- The directory shows organisation verification and federation portal state separately. The review queue's Approved filter describes applications, not formal membership or recognition. Organisation detail gives eligible publishers access to existing open, pause and resume controls. An approved application does not send email or open a portal automatically.
- Activity includes recorded application submissions, review decisions and portal changes. Public access logs and player case histories are not mixed into the central organisation feed. A record's activity is scoped to that organisation.
- Network acceptance: submit a request, find it by name, approve it, follow the link to the same organisation, verify the portal isn't open, publish explicitly, and verify the directory and activity reflect the change. Pause and resume the portal from the same organisation. Also confirm an intake-only reviewer cannot publish and a federation operator cannot open the network dashboard by URL.
