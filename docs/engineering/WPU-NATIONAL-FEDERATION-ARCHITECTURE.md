# National federation architecture — WPU-001 extension
Approved direction: one global identity, multiple affiliations. BPF is the intended first pilot, not an established or recognised federation by virtue of this document.

Existing identity, authentication, privacy and registration requirements remain unchanged. country_code on persons means residence, never nationality or representation. Global player codes must not encode country or organisation. Existing organisation memberships and scoped roles are reused; global individual membership must have no organisation_id. No affiliation grants administrative authority or access to private player data.

Minimum foundation: typed organisations with immutable IDs; directed, independently evidenced affiliation, ownership, recognition and operational authority relationships; separate domestic recognition and WPU affiliation records with pending defaults and effective/expiry dates. No automatic hierarchy permissions. Multiple organisation memberships are supported; eligibility and credentials are distinct future records. Federation profile metadata (branding, contacts, domains, languages, timezone, currency, governance documents, representatives, policies and operational status) belongs to the later federation module. No BPF production record or recognition assertion is authorised by this directive.

Release gate: test one player with two organisation memberships, unchanged global ID, no implicit recognition or authority, and private-by-default profile. Organisation-specific API access must be explicitly authorised and tested before any federation portal is exposed. Keep WPU-001 registration/login/Passport delivery as the active milestone; defer federation administration, eligibility adjudication, credentials and custom domains. Do not deploy schema changes before isolated D1 tests and migration review.
