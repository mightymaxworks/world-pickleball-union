# WPU Federation Competition Standard

This is the shared operating standard for participating federations. Numerical point tables, tier approval criteria, qualifying routes and competition-specific allowances require WPU approval and publication before use. A published event page can include both sanctioned and unsanctioned divisions. Only the sanction badge on a division confirms its tier.

## What federations can run

Clubs and federations can run local open draws, development divisions, challengers and advanced competitions. They operate their own events, branding, scheduling and prizes. WPU approves sanctioning and the points tier per division under one standard. A competition can mix tiers. A venue or prize pool does not determine points.

## How to get sanctioned

**Local Open (T5).** A verified organisation’s authorised organiser completes the required event and division details and publishes. A complete open-entry division gets T5 automatically. There is no review form. This release does not retrospectively sanction old publications. A private draft does not confer sanction.

**Higher tiers (T4–T1).** In Event Studio, save the tournament, then open **Request a higher tier** under Competition. Select the target tier and divisions. Explain the expected field and how players qualify, who is officiating, and how results will be verified. WPU staff decide in WPU Control based on the requested level, the integrity and suitability of the field and eligibility rules, readiness for officiating and results verification, and the evidence of delivery capacity. WPU reviews higher tiers in this release instead of inventing automatic thresholds. The organiser sees any requested changes in Event Studio.

The decision applies to the submitted division, date, time zone, venue and entry rules. Revisions to those details need a new review before an approved higher tier carries to the revised public page. A pending request never counts as approval. A Local Open division may keep its T5 public badge while a higher tier is under review. International attendance, event size and prizes do not on their own qualify for a higher tier.

## Entry and seeding

- Use WPU ranking points for ranking-based eligibility, never WPU Skill Rating, DUPR or another provider’s skill rating. A skill rating estimates ability and is guidance only. Qualifying conditions such as age or roster scope are separate from skill.
- Open-entry divisions can have no points minimum. Offer published on-court qualifying routes at designated events when places are available.
- For ranked entry, identify the relevant WPU leaderboard, the minimum and the cutoff before opening entries. Apply the same snapshot and rule to everyone in the division. Plainly distinguish eligibility, selection in a capped draw, and seeding. Ranking determines seeding only when the published rules use it. A later ranking update must not silently change an accepted entry.
- Men’s Doubles, Women’s Doubles and Mixed Doubles are distinct competition categories. Set the category in Event Studio and use its matching WPU leaderboard if a points cutoff applies. Keep Open Doubles separate when there is no specified gender category.
- In doubles and mixed doubles, the entry is a pair but the ranking is individual. Both partners must independently meet the minimum on the relevant leaderboard. Do not pool or average points. Use the separate team ledger for team ranking rules.

## Results and awards

Match results and published tiebreaks determine final placement. All WPU-sanctioned divisions award points under the approved tier and placement schedule, with small-field adjustments where applicable. Only verified results can post. A doubles or mixed doubles placement earns the full approved award for each player individually, independent of prize splits or future partner choices. Team awards post to a separate ledger. A correction must leave an audit trail and cannot award points twice.

## Release boundary

Event Studio stores and previews divisions and entry rules. This release supports automatic T5 sanctioning and higher-tier requests and decisions. Registration, ranking snapshots, qualifying decisions and point awards are not activated.
