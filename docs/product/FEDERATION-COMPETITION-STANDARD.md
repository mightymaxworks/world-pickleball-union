# WPU Federation Competition Standard

This is the shared operating standard for participating federations. Numerical point tables, tier approval criteria, qualifying routes and competition-specific allowances require WPU approval and publication before use. A published event page is not evidence of sanction.

## What federations can run

Clubs and federations can run local open draws, development divisions, challengers and advanced competitions. They operate their own events, branding, scheduling and prizes. WPU approves sanctioning and the points tier per division under one standard. A competition can mix tiers. A venue or prize pool does not determine points.

## Entry and seeding

- Use WPU ranking points for ranking-based eligibility, never WPU Skill Rating, DUPR or another provider’s skill rating. A skill rating estimates ability and is guidance only. Qualifying conditions such as age or roster scope are separate from skill.
- Open-entry divisions can have no points minimum. Offer published on-court qualifying routes at designated events when places are available.
- For ranked entry, identify the relevant WPU leaderboard, the minimum and the cutoff before opening entries. Apply the same snapshot and rule to everyone in the division. Plainly distinguish eligibility, selection in a capped draw, and seeding. Ranking determines seeding only when the published rules use it. A later ranking update must not silently change an accepted entry.
- In doubles and mixed doubles, the entry is a pair but the ranking is individual. Both partners must independently meet the minimum on the relevant leaderboard. Do not pool or average points. Use the separate team ledger for team ranking rules.

## Results and awards

Match results and published tiebreaks determine final placement. All WPU-sanctioned divisions award points under the approved tier and placement schedule, with small-field adjustments where applicable. Only verified results can post. A doubles or mixed doubles placement earns the full approved award for each player individually, independent of prize splits or future partner choices. Team awards post to a separate ledger. A correction must leave an audit trail and cannot award points twice.

## Release boundary

Event Studio can store and preview divisions and their proposed entry rules. Publication makes the information page public only. Sanction approval, registration, ranking snapshots, qualifying decisions and point awards are not activated by this release.
