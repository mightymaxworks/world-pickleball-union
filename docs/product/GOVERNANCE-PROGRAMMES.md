# WPU governance and programme register — v1

## Governance direction
AI-native organisation, minimal routine human intervention with explicit human constitutional, ethical, appeal and consequential authority. WPU's ultimate institutional recognition and constitutional structure remain undetermined. Do not represent partner discussions as executed agreements.

## Equipment certification
Proposed accessible annual paddle-model certification: USD 50 per model per year; NFC-linked validity for one year; optional on-site testing. Published objective standards, evidence, renewal, suspension, independent appeal and public certificate verification required. Singapore Institute of Technology backing is proposed/subject to formal confirmation, not asserted as contracted.

## Education and development
Coach education and upskilling, youth pathways, accessibility, cross-sport participation, marina pickleball and sailing–pickleball athlete exchange. Maintain separate programme records and metrics, integrated with WPU identity and organisation master data. No implication of formal world sailing endorsement.

## Research and institutional collaboration
Potential AI governance living-laboratory collaboration with MAGIC JusTICe Lab, JSW Law and JUSTECH; subject to agreements and independent review. WPU remains viable independently. Research data requires safeguards, independent evaluation and consent.

## Commercial independence
Sponsorship, equipment testing and optional services may fund free tournament/ranking infrastructure; no sale of ranking points, certification outcomes or governance influence.
